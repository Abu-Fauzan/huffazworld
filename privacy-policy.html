Privacy Policy HuffazWorld Effective date: August 31, 2026 HuffazWorld is an online platform for Qur'an memorization, Islamic learning, community interaction, and related educational activities. This Privacy Policy explains how HuffazWorld collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the HuffazWorld website or Android application. 1. Information We Collect Depending on the features you use, HuffazWorld may collect or process: Account information: information used to create and authenticate your account, such as your name, email address, username, and authentication information. Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile, such as profile details and educational or learning information. User-generated content: posts, comments, messages, group or classroom content, and other information you choose to submit through the platform. Learning information: Qur'an memorization, revision, classroom, or progress information that you choose to record or use within HuffazWorld. Technical information: information needed to operate and secure the service, which may include device, browser, application, network, and diagnostic information. Notifications: information necessary to deliver app notifications and service-related messages when notification features are enabled. 2. How We Use Information We use information to: create and manage user accounts; authenticate users and protect accounts; provide profiles, posts, comments, messaging, groups, classrooms, and community features; provide Qur'an memorization and learning features; save and display learning progress and other information requested by the user; send service-related notifications; maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve HuffazWorld; and prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and other harmful activity. 3. How Information Is Stored and Processed HuffazWorld uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Firebase services, to provide authentication, database, hosting and related application functionality. Information may therefore be transmitted to and processed by service providers supporting HuffazWorld. We do not sell personal or sensitive user data. 4. Sharing of Information Information may be visible to other HuffazWorld users when you intentionally publish it through social, group, classroom, profile, or messaging features. Information may also be processed by service providers that operate infrastructure required to provide HuffazWorld. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce our terms, protect users, or protect the security and integrity of the service. 5. User-Generated Content You are responsible for information and content that you choose to publish or send through HuffazWorld. Please avoid publishing sensitive personal information that you do not want other users to see. 6. Security We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between your device and supported services is protected using appropriate transport security such as HTTPS where available. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. 7. Data Retention We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain security, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Delete Your HuffazWorld Account This page provides the external account-deletion request process for HuffazWorld. How to request deletion Send an email from the email address associated with your HuffazWorld account. Use the subject HuffazWorld Account Deletion Request. Include your HuffazWorld name or account email so we can identify the account. Request account deletion by email We will review and process valid requests and delete associated personal data subject to legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory retention requirements.
